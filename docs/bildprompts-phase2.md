- **Inhalt:** 15 Rümpfe, Zeilen = Stufe 4..6, Spalten = Späher, Kampfpanzer, Schwerer Panzer, Artillerie, Unterstützer
- **Umfang:** 15 Rümpfe (3 Zeilen x 5 Spalten)
- **Stilreferenz anhängen:** ja
- **Prüfen:** Draufsicht, alle nach RECHTS, nichts abgeschnitten, kein Text.

**Kopiere diesen Prompt komplett:**

```
Hand-painted gouache and ink illustration with visible brush texture and slightly uneven hand-drawn dark outlines, in a bright, warm, natural storybook style. Fresh, saturated but harmonious colors, clean readable silhouettes, friendly and a little rugged, never dirty or grim. No neon, no glossy 3D render, no photorealism, no lens flares, no glow effects, no airbrushed gradients. Practical armored vehicles have riveted plates, visible hatches, tracks or wheels and slightly asymmetric details.

Vehicle hull sheet. A grid of 3 rows by 5 columns, 15 armored vehicle HULLS WITHOUT turrets, every vehicle pointing to the RIGHT. Rows from top to bottom: row 1: tier 4 (Warden: tidy, professionally built, uniform olive-green with white stripe markings); row 2: tier 5 (Striker: sleek, angular armor, dark blue-gray with orange accent panels); row 3: tier 6 (Bulwark: massive layered plating, desert tan with dark brown trim). The columns left to right: (1) fast light SCOUT, (2) balanced MEDIUM tank, (3) heavy slow HEAVY tank with thick plating, (4) long-range ARTILLERY carrier, (5) SUPPORT vehicle with antennas and repair arms. Each hull has an EMPTY round turret ring in the middle (turret NOT drawn). Higher tiers look larger, more armored and more elaborate; every column keeps its own clear silhouette across the rows. Keep the same scale ratio as the style reference.

Background: one plain, solid, very dark near-black color (#0a0b0e), completely flat, no floor, no ground, no horizon, no vignette, no shadow on the background. Every object is fully visible and complete, separated from its neighbours by generous empty margin, arranged evenly on a clean grid, with no overlaps and nothing cut off at the image edge. View: STRICTLY top-down, orthographic, as if photographed from straight above like a satellite or blueprint view. No perspective, no side view, no three-quarter view. Lighting: soft light from the top-left. Do NOT paint cast shadows on the ground; only natural shading on the objects. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no frame, no border, no people, no user interface. Square 1:1 image, highest resolution available.
```
