## Tasks, in this order

### 1. Drones become real companions (the owner cannot see them in the game)
Today drones are only items that add a bit of damage (`shared/rules/character.ts`, `def.slotType === 'drone'`); there is no drone in the world. Implement real drones: 8 types from `shared/data/items.ts` (`guardian`, `sentinel`, `repair_bot`, `recon`, `arc_drone`, `sapper`, `interceptor`, `aegis`) x 5 tiers, up to `droneSlots` per vehicle. Sim (`shared/sim`, deterministic): each equipped drone is a small companion entity (new `EntityKind` `drone`, owner id, type, tier) that follows the owner in a formation (orbit, wing, trail: formation per type, slot index decides the position) and behaves by type: guardian absorbs a share of incoming damage and shows a shield flash; sentinel auto-fires a light gun at the owner's target; repair_bot slowly repairs hull/armor out of combat; recon widens the radar/aggro-awareness of the owner (radar range bonus in the HUD); arc_drone chains small damage to enemies near the target; sapper lays short-lived slowing mines behind the vehicle; interceptor shoots down incoming enemy artillery shells/telegraphs partly; aegis gives a periodic short shield. Stats scale with tier and the item's rarity/upgrade level; their damage replaces the flat `dmgAdd` hack so total power stays near the old balance. Drones die/despawn with the owner, are not targetable by normal targeting, are not counted for capacity, and do not spawn in safe zones' hub clutter (still shown, but harmless). Snapshot: keep it cheap (send a compact `drones` list on the owner entity instead of one full entity each if that is smaller; measure). Client: draw them in `src/render/vectorDraw.ts` as small animated quadcopter shapes (use the existing `slot_drone` icon as base sprite tinted per type, plus a per-type accent: shield bubble, repair beam, scan ring, arc lightning, mine blips), visible to other players too, with cheap shadows. Inventory/garage UI shows what each drone does. Tests: formation positions deterministic, each type's effect, despawn rules, replay determinism, no snapshot bloat. Re-run the balance pipeline and keep the guard tests green.

### 2. Shots always hit (permanent rule, no switch)
Design decision from the owner, final: when you attack, the shot always lands; misses make attacking pointless. This is a permanent game rule, NOT optional: no feature flag, no setting, no toggle. Implement guided projectiles for the player's main cannon and secondary weapons: every attack has a target (the locked target, or with auto-fire / fire button the nearest valid enemy in weapon range is picked automatically and locked), and the projectile steers toward it with a capped turn rate, so it only misses if the target dies, enters a safe zone, becomes invulnerable or leaves the weapon's max range plus a grace margin. Only a shot with no valid target at all flies straight. Enemies' shots at their target follow the same rule. Applies to PvE, PvP and the arena (write it down in `docs/GDD.md`). The angle damage model (front 0.7 / side 1.0 / rear 1.4), crits, ammo types and armor rules stay unchanged. Mortars/artillery keep their telegraph mechanic (the ground ring is the warning, the shell still lands there). Keep it deterministic (`shared/sim`), cheap in the sim, and visually clear (a slightly curved flight is fine). Tests: a shot at a strafing target always hits; a shot at a target that enters a safe zone does not; auto-fire picks and keeps a target; replay determinism. Re-run the balance pipeline: the hit rate went up, so damage or reload must be re-tuned to keep the TTK bands (`test/balance.test.ts` stays green).

### 3. Visible feedback for weapons, ammo, modules and statuses

Add a settings toggle "Reduced effects" that lowers particle caps (the setting exists in accessibility options; honour it everywhere). Cap total particles; add a test that the particle system never exceeds its cap and a frame-time budget check in the e2e script if feasible.

### 4. Combat readability: HUD, target panel, threat indicators
Make fights readable on a phone: enemy/player health+armor bars with distance-based level of detail (no name text beyond a distance, bars only when damaged or targeted), floating damage numbers (merged per target per 200 ms, crit/rear styles), a compact target panel (name, level, class/archetype, hull/armor, status effects, which ammo is effective against it, estimated kills-to-go), off-screen threat arrows for aggressive enemies and for the player's current target, loot/ore pickup feedback, a kill feed line for notable kills (elite, boss), clear low-health warning. Make all of it respect the UI scale and colour-blind settings. Keep DOM updates throttled (no per-frame React setState). Add tests for the pure logic (damage number merging, LOD rules, threat selection).

### 5. First-hour onboarding
