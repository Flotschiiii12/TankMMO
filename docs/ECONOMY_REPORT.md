# Economy and progression report

Generated by `npm run econ-sim` (100 simulated players per style, 200 kills per hour of play, 2 h per day).

## Before and after

Measured with the same simulator, 100 players, 200 kills per hour:

| | level 30 | level 60 | level 100 | all four items +15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| target | ~20 h | ~120 h | ~500 h | very grindy |
| before (XP curve 80 n^2.1, gate rewards up to 220k XP, upgrade costs independent of item tier) | 26 h | 94 h | 220 h | never reached the cost in a useful time (upgrades were trivial: all items +10 in 8 h) |
| after (this change) | 20 h | 124 h | 505 h | 1,129 h (81 casual weeks, 32 dedicated weeks); hypothetical payer 961 h |

What changed (all numbers are constants you can tune):
- `shared/rules/progression.ts`: XP curve `7.7 * n^2.7` instead of `80 * n^2.1` (`XP_CURVE_BASE`, `XP_CURVE_EXPONENT`). The steeper exponent is what makes the last 40 levels take four times as long as the first 60.
- `shared/data/dungeons.ts`: rewards of the three top gates cut (Frozen Foundry XP 18k to 9.5k, Glowworm Sump XP 26k to 11.5k, Iron Crown Vault 150k scrap / 220k XP to 70k / 21k). Before, one gate run was worth ten minutes of killing at level 90, which made the free daily keys the main XP source and the extra key packs a real advantage.
- `shared/rules/character.ts`: `upgradeCost(level, safe, itemTier)`: scrap x1.45 and ore/fragments x1.25 per item tier above 1 (`UPGRADE_TIER_SCRAP_GROWTH`, `UPGRADE_TIER_MATERIAL_GROWTH`). Before, +15 cost the same for a tier 1 and a tier 8 item. The client shows the tier-aware cost.
- The simulator is more realistic: time spent in gates is not spent killing; the real +10..+15 failure rule (sensible players use safe mode from +10); new item tiers every 12 levels restart the upgrades; the real death penalty (10 % of the scrap on hand, `gameServer.ts`); crafting; fragments as a resource; the endgame grind after level 100.

Fairness check ("very grindy but fair"): the hypothetical payer buys the only premium products that touch progression - "repair free day" (no death penalty) and gate key packs (+3 gate runs per day, only used when a gate pays better than killing) - every day. Levelling is not faster at all (<1 %), the endgame gear about 15 % faster (the death penalty is the only thing that matters there). The premium currency stays disabled (`FLAGS.premiumCurrencyEnabled = false`); nothing in the shop sells stats, loot or upgrades.

Open points: there is no repair cost or durability system in the game - the only death cost is the 10 % scrap loss; if you want a stronger sink at the endgame, that is the place. Market (auction) trades are not simulated: they only move scrap between players and burn the 5 % tax.

## Best gear: how long does it take?

"Best gear" = the vehicle of tier 10 and all four fitted items upgraded to +15 (the strongest state the game allows). Weeks assume a casual player (14 h/week) and a dedicated one (35 h/week).

| milestone | free player (h) | casual weeks | dedicated weeks | hypothetical payer (h) | payer saves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all items +10 | 349.0 | 24.9 | 10.0 | 350.0 | -0 % |
| tier 10 vehicle | 404.0 | 28.9 | 11.5 | 405.0 | -0 % |
| all items +15 | 1129.0 | 80.6 | 32.3 | 961.0 | 15 % |

The payer model buys the only things the (disabled) premium shop sells that touch progression: "repair free day" (no death penalty) and gate key packs (+3 gate runs per day). Inventory slots, garage slots and skins do not change income. The result must show that paying shortens the grind only slightly.
